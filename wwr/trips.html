Beginner River Rafting — Half Day

Duration: 4 hours • Difficulty: Easy (family friendly)

Perfect for first-timers and families. Includes safety briefing, all gear (helmet, life jacket), guided raft trip on Class I–II rapids, and a riverside snack break.

  • What to expect: gentle rapids, scenic canyon views, swimming opportunities

Rapids & Canyon — Full Day

Duration: 8–9 hours • Difficulty: Moderate (experienced beginners ok)

An action-packed day on the river: bigger rapids, lunch on a scenic beach, short guided hikes along canyon viewpoints. Recommended for people who enjoyed our half-day trip and want more.

  • What to expect: Class III rapids, scenic stops, photo opportunities

Multi-Day Wilderness Expedition — 3 Days

Duration: 3 days / 2 nights • Difficulty: Challenging (fit & prepared)

Immersive expedition down remote sections of the river with overnight riverside camping. Includes camp setup, guided whitewater sections, wilderness skills, and all meals.

  • What to expect: multi-class rapids, portages, camp cooking, starry nights
